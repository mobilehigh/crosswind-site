Crosswind · Android

Privacy policy

Effective 17 October 2026

Crosswind is an endless flying game for Android, made by mobilehigh. This page explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: the game collects nothing about you.

What Crosswind collects

Nothing. Crosswind does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information or usage data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party services.

What stays on your phone

To keep your progress between flights, the game saves the following in its own storage on your device:

  • your points, unlocked aircraft and upgrades
  • your best distance for each aircraft
  • your settings, such as sound and Relax mode

This information never leaves your phone through Crosswind. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes it. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include it in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account's settings, not by Crosswind.

Internet access

The whole game is inside the app and works offline. Crosswind does not connect to the internet while you play. The app lists Android's standard internet permission because the web view it uses to draw the game expects it, but Crosswind does not use it to send or receive anything.

Children

Crosswind does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the contact email on Crosswind's Google Play page, or open an issue at github.com/mobilehigh/crosswind-site/issues.
